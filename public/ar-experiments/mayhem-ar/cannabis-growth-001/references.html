Observed growth.
Reconstructed in 3D.

This animation follows three individual plants filmed by Dutch Passion. Their videos guide the changing proportions, branching silhouette, leaves and flower clusters. Each variety has a separate model and timeline.

This is a reference-based artistic reconstruction, not an exact scan. The source is edited and mainly viewed from one direction. Hidden branches, rear surfaces, some early stages and movement between sampled frames are inferred. Plants of the same variety can differ.

What is modelled

Branches attach to a hierarchy of nodes. Individual leaves emerge and unfold, and the flower clusters develop as overlapping small structures with narrow sugar leaves, fine pistils and surface detail. Reported height annotations inform approximate adult scale; the size control lets you change the AR scale.

Female cannabis inflorescences have complex branching anatomy. The model uses that structure rather than a single petalled bloom. Spitzer-Rimon et al., Architecture and Florogenesis in Female Cannabis sativa Plants (2019) ↗

Materials & leaf movement

The original botanical texture atlases were generated for this project. It supplies green and purple leaf and flower surfaces. Normal and roughness maps add fine relief and varied reflections; curved serrated meshes, overlapping bracts and three-dimensional details supply the larger forms. Crossed transparent flower-detail layers add fine hairs and sugar-leaf silhouettes around the modeled volume. These materials are artistic assets, not measured scans of the filmed plants.

The Sun & moon controls add a simulated cycle of leaf lifting and relaxing. Its intensity and speed are adjustable. This motion pauses with growth playback and is not a measured reconstruction of the recordings’ lighting conditions. Turning it off retains the developmental animation.

AR continuity

Detected ground placement and manual placement are supported. Move the plant with both joysticks, adjust its starting height, and set the independent ground cut-off. Hide controls or choose Grow full screen to view the animation unobstructed. A lost tracking pose pauses playback; resume after checking alignment.

Browser checks use actual WebGL with emulated XR poses. Physical Android Chrome alignment, lighting and performance require testing on the phone.
